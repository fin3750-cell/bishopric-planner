Bishopric Planner is a small, free web app that a ward bishopric uses to plan sacrament meeting speakers, track callings and keep a ward calendar. By signing in, you agree to these terms. It is not an official product of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Who may use it

  • Only people the bishopric invites, signed in with the Google account the bishopric's Google Sheet is shared with.
  • Don't share your sign-in or let others use the app through your account.
  • The bishopric can change or remove anyone's access at any time.

Using ward information

  • The member information in the app is for Church use only. Use it only for the ward planning the app is for.
  • Don't copy member information out of the app or the Sheet for other purposes, and don't share it with people who don't have access.
  • What you add (calendar items, proposed callings, notes) is saved in the bishopric's Sheet, and everyone with access can see it. Keep it accurate, brief and kind.
