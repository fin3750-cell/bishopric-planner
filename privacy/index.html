Bishopric Planner

Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Bishopric Planner (fin3750-cell.github.io/bishopric-planner) is a small web app that a ward bishopric uses to plan sacrament meeting speakers, track callings and keep a ward calendar. Only people the bishopric invites use it. It is not an official product of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it uses, stores, shares and deletes that data.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets (permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets). The app reads and writes one Google Sheet: the bishopric's planner Sheet. It also reads, and never changes, the stake calendar Sheet whose link the bishopric saves in the app. The planner Sheet holds ward members' names and ages (members age 8 and older, from the ward's membership records), sacrament meeting talks, callings, calendar items, notes, and the lists of people invited to use the app with their email addresses. The app does not open, list or change any other spreadsheet in your account.
  • Your email address (permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email). The app reads the email address of the Google account you sign in with.
  • A sign-in token. Google gives the app a token that lasts about an hour, so it can make these requests for you.
  • The app does not access your Gmail, Drive files, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How the app uses that data

  • Sheet data is used only to show and update the ward's plans in the app: who to ask to speak, talks given, callings and releases in progress, and the ward calendar.
  • Your email address is used only to recognize who is signed in, so the app shows the right screens: the whole app for the bishopric, or callings, the calendar and member names and ages for other invited people. When a member of the bishopric links their account ("Which one are you?"), their email address is saved on the Bishopric list in the planner Sheet.
  • Things you add, such as a calendar item or a proposed calling, are marked with your initials, your name or your email address, so others can see who added them.
  • The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How the app stores that data

  • Ward information is stored only in the bishopric's Google Sheet, in Google's systems. The app has no server or database of its own, and nothing is sent to its developer.
  • On your device, the app saves only a few settings in this browser: the planner Sheet's id, the app's Google sign-in id, your initials, and two choices from the Settings screen (the look-back window in years and the minimum age). It also keeps a copy of its own files (code, styles and icons) so it opens quickly.
  • Member information and the sign-in token are kept only in the open page's memory. They are not saved on your device and are gone when the app is closed.
  • All requests to Google use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How the app shares that data

  • The app does not share Google user data with anyone. The only people who can see the ward's information are those the bishopric shares the Google Sheet with; Google's sharing settings enforce that, in the app and in Google Sheets.
  • The app sends data only to Google (Google sign-in, Google's email address service and the Google Sheets service). It uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, web fonts or other outside services, and it sets no cookies of its own.
  • Bishopric Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Information stays in the bishopric's Sheet until the bishopric changes it. The app does not delete rows; it marks a mistake as void. The Sheet's owner can delete rows or the whole Sheet in Google Sheets at any time.
  • To have information about you corrected or deleted, ask the bishopric.
  • To stop the app's access to your Google account, sign out in the app and remove Bishopric Planner on your Google Account's third-party access page.
  • To delete the settings saved on your device, clear this site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no member information. GitHub may record technical information, such as IP addresses, when the files are downloaded, under GitHub's privacy statement. Google sign-in uses Google's own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions go to the ward bishopric.
