Bishopric Planner

Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Bishopric Planner is a small web app that a ward bishopric uses to plan sacrament meeting speakers, track callings and keep a ward calendar. Only people the bishopric invites use it. It is not an official product of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Where the information is kept

  • The ward's information (member names and ages, talks, callings, calendar items and notes) is kept in one Google Sheet that belongs to the bishopric. The app has no server or database of its own.
  • The member list includes ward members age 8 and older. It comes from the ward's membership records and is for Church use only.
  • The Sheet's sharing settings decide who can see the information, in the app or in Google Sheets. The app only lets a person see what Google lets that person open. Some invited people see only part of the app (callings, the calendar and member names and ages). That limits what the app shows; it is not a separate copy of the data.
  • The app's own files (its code, styles and icons) are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no member information.

What the app asks Google for when you sign in

  • Google Sheets. The app uses this permission only to read and write the ward's planner Sheet, and to read (never change) the stake calendar Sheet whose link the bishopric saves in the app. It does not open, list or change any other spreadsheet.
  • Your email address. The app uses it only to recognize who is signed in, so it can show the right screens. When a member of the bishopric links their account ("Which one are you?"), their email address is saved on the Bishopric list in the Sheet. The bishopric adds the email addresses of other invited people to the Sheet themselves.
  • Google gives the app a sign-in pass that lasts about an hour. The app keeps it only while the page is open. It is never saved on your device or sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Things you add (a calendar item, a proposed calling) are marked with your initials, your name or your email address, so others can see who added them.

What is saved on your device

  • A few settings, in this browser only: the planner Sheet's id, the app's Google sign-in id, your initials, and two choices from the Settings screen (the look-back window in years and the minimum age).
  • A copy of the app's own files, so it opens quickly and works with a weak connection.
  • The app does not save member information on your device. It reads it from the Sheet when it opens.

Who the app talks to

  • Google, and nothing else: Google sign-in, Google's service that tells the app your email address, and the Google Sheets service.
  • GitHub Pages, to download the app's files. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when the files are downloaded, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, web fonts or other services. The app sets no cookies of its own. Google sign-in uses Google's cookies, under Google's privacy policy.

How the information is used and shared

  • Only to run the app's features for the bishopric and the people it invites.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone outside the people the Sheet is shared with. The app sends nothing to its developer; there is no server that could receive it.
  • Bishopric Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changing or removing information

  • Information stays in the Sheet until the bishopric changes it. The app does not delete rows; it marks a mistake as void. The Sheet's owner can delete rows in Google Sheets.
  • To have information about you corrected or removed, ask the bishopric.
  • To sign out, use Sign out in the app. To remove the app's access to your Google account, go to your Google Account's third-party access page and remove Bishopric Planner.
  • To remove the settings saved on your device, clear this site's data in your browser.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions go to the ward bishopric.
